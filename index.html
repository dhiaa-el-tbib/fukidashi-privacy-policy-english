Summary

  • We have no servers and collect no data about you. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.
  • Screen images are processed on your phone only. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • No text leaves your phone unless you choose the Gemini engine yourself and enter your own API key.

Screen capture

When you tap the floating bubble, the app captures a single frame of the screen with your permission (MediaProjection). It crops the area you select and reads its text on the phone with Google ML Kit. The image is removed from memory once the translation is shown; it is never written to storage or sent over the internet. Between taps the app captures nothing.

Translation engines

  • Fast engine (ML Kit): translates on the phone without internet. Each language model is downloaded once from Google's servers.
  • High quality (TranslateGemma): optional, translates on the phone without internet. The model is downloaded once from Hugging Face when you ask for it.
  • Gemini (optional): only when you choose it, the recognized bubble text (never the image), together with the last few bubbles of the same session and the matching terms of your glossary, is sent to the Google Gemini API using your own key. This data is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy; Google may use content sent through the free tier of the Gemini API to improve its services. Do not use Gemini with text you do not want to share.

When models are downloaded, the servers involved (Google or Hugging Face) see your IP address, as with any download from the internet.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your settings (languages and engine) and your glossary.
  • Your Gemini key, if you enter one, encrypted with a key from the Android Keystore and excluded from backups.
  • The language models you chose to download.

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also delete the key, the models and the glossary from inside the app at any time.

Third-party data retention

We keep no data about you because we have no servers. The app does use third-party services, and each keeps data under its own policy:

  • Google Gemini API (only if you choose it): receives the bubble text and the context described above, with your key. Google retains and processes this data under the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy. On the free tier, inputs and outputs may be retained and reviewed by humans to improve Google's services; on the paid tier they are kept for a limited time for abuse monitoring only. The account and key are yours, and you control this data from your Google account.
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition and fast translation): processing happens on the phone and no images or text are sent. ML Kit may contact Google's servers to download models and their updates, and sends anonymous technical metrics about the performance and use of the service (such as device type and processing time) under the ML Kit terms.
  • Hugging Face (high quality model, only if you download it): sees your IP address and the download request, and keeps its logs under the Hugging Face privacy policy.
  • Google Play: collects installation and crash data under its own policy, not through the app.

To stop sending any data to Gemini, choose another engine or delete your key in the app. To delete everything on your phone, delete the data in Settings or uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: to show the floating bubble and the translation over your reader app.
  • Screen capture and foreground service: to capture a frame only when you tap the bubble.
  • Notifications: for the running notification and its stop button.
  • Internet: to download models, and for Gemini if you choose it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data about any user.
